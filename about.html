About World GeoData

This app was created by two students on the Software Engineering Bootcamp at Founders and Coders, London, UK.

Whether you are a student, researcher, or just curious about the world, World GeoData offers a user-friendly interface to explore various aspects of countries, including their area, population, capital cities, and currencies.

The app is drawn from two API sources: REST Countries and Open-Meteo. The country data is fetched from the REST Countries API, which provides comprehensive information about countries worldwide. The weather data is obtained from the Open-Meteo API, which offers accurate and up-to-date weather forecasts for various locations.
